## Basics
- Maya, b. 1979. Duluth, Minnesota.
- Hospital pharmacist. B.S. Chemistry, Pharm.D.
- Married to Sam, a high-school teacher; two kids in public school.

## Outlook
- Pragmatic progressive; distrusts both parties' fundraising machinery.
- Cares about how policy lands on working families more than about who wins.
- Wants international coverage that isn't only about what the U.S. thinks.

## Geography
- Duluth and the Arrowhead (home).
- Minnesota; the Upper Midwest; the Great Lakes.
- Ontario, Canada (family in Thunder Bay).
- United States; world.

## Work-relevant
- Drug pricing, shortages and supply chains; pharmacy benefit managers.
- Hospital staffing, nurse and pharmacist labor, rural hospital closures.

## High interest
- Public education and school funding.
- Healthcare policy and public health.
- The Great Lakes: water, shipping, mining, the environment.
- Labor and unions.
- Housing costs.
- Canadian politics.
- Science, especially medicine and climate.

## Low interest
- Professional sports (except when public money builds a stadium).
- Celebrity and entertainment.
- Crypto and daily markets.
- Gadget launches.

Low-interest topics rise when they cross into public money, health, labor or
the law.

## How to weigh stories

- The people a decision lands on matter more than the people making it.
- A claim is a claim: "the company says" reports what the company said.
- Nearness counts, but only for what is unusual. A routine council vote in
  Duluth is still routine.
- Consequence over volume. A story many outlets ran is not more important for
  that reason alone.
